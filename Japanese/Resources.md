# Japanese Resources — compact discovery and request index

This is not a source library, vocabulary tracker, or standing book list. The
active Japanese curriculum dossier receives one verified locator only when its
concept needs one.

## Vocabulary

- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) — owner of Japanese vocabulary and mined
  cards. The vault records learner-reported mining events, not a second word
  database.

## Grammar and learner sources

- [Yoku.bi](https://yoku.bi) — optional grammar explanation/reference.
- [Imabi](https://imabi.org/) — optional grammar explanation/reference.
- When a lesson needs a book, ask the tutor for one; the resource skill selects
  and verifies the right level/edition on demand. A learner-supplied book is
  welcome.

## Reading and immersion discovery

- [Yatsu](https://app.yatsu.moe/) — optional reader/lookup support; verify
  parser output against the page.
- [Jimaku](https://www.jimaku.cc/) — subtitle lookup.
- [jiten.moe](https://jiten.moe/) — difficulty/media discovery.

The learner chooses the novel. No title is silently substituted. Raw page
screenshots and passages are transient inputs; public records contain only
short summaries and evidence links.
