- 2026-09-22 jp-godan-potential - **SECOND LAPSE, same root cause.** Cold recall produced かく→かける ✅, たべる→たべられる ✅, but まつ→ませる ❌ (expected まてる) and よぶ→dictionary form ❌ (expected よべる). The t-row got the s-row's e column, i.e. the consonant changed — the identical failure shape as 2026-09-14 (まつ→まねる). Not a memory lapse: the gojuuon-row lookup is not automatic. The learner's frequency observation about the b-row form was treated as a real observation, not an error; b-row/m-row potentials are regular but comparatively rare in speech. Outcome `miss` at rung 0. Per the review skill, two or more lapses -> **re-encode differently next session**: switch from potential-form rules to a gojuuon-grid lookup drill (draw the five rows, mark the e column, read the answer off the grid).
