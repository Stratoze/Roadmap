# Editions and Sources

> Goal: use pieces that are easy to find in high-quality editions.
> Public domain first, but standard 20th-century works are allowed when they are easy to buy and teach unique skills.

## Source priority

1. IMSLP public domain urtext or reputable old edition
2. Henle, Bärenreiter, Wiener Urtext
3. ABRSM, Schirmer, Peters, Alfred Masterwork
4. RCM Celebration Series for graded convenience
5. Official licensed scores for anime and game music
6. Community transcriptions only after checking against a recording

## Composer edition suggestions

- **Bach** — Henle, Bärenreiter, ABRSM, Peters. *Avoid random free editions with bad fingerings.*
- **Handel** — Bärenreiter, Henle, ABRSM. *Suites and selected movements.*
- **Scarlatti** — Henle, Ricordi, ABRSM, Schirmer. *Selected sonatas.*
- **C.P.E. Bach** — Bärenreiter, Henle. *Selected sonatas and solfeggio.*
- **Haydn** — Wiener Urtext, Henle, ABRSM. *Sonatas.*
- **Mozart** — Henle, Bärenreiter, Wiener Urtext, ABRSM. *Sonatas.*
- **Clementi** — Schirmer, ABRSM, Peters. *Sonatinas and sonatas.*
- **Beethoven** — Henle, ABRSM, Schirmer, Wiener Urtext. *Sonatas.*
- **Schubert** — Henle, Bärenreiter, Schirmer. *Impromptus and sonatas.*
- **Mendelssohn** — Henle, Peters, Schirmer. *Songs without Words.*
- **Chopin** — Henle, Peters, Schirmer, Paderewski. *Nocturnes, Waltzes, Ballades, Etudes.*
- **Schumann** — Henle, Peters, Breitkopf. *Kinderszenen, Fantasiestücke, Kreisleriana.*
- **Liszt** — Henle, Editio Musica Budapest, Schirmer, Peters. *Consolations, Sonetto, Sonata, Etudes.*
- **Brahms** — Henle, Peters, Breitkopf. *Intermezzi, Ballades, Sonata.*
- **Debussy** — Henle, Durand, Peters. *Arabesques, Preludes, Images.*
- **Ravel** — Durand, Henle. *Sonatine, Miroirs, Gaspard.*
- **Fauré** — Henle, Peters. *Nocturnes, Barcarolles.*
- **Rachmaninoff** — Boosey and Hawkes, Schirmer. *Preludes, Etudes-tableaux.*
- **Scriabin** — Henle, Edition Peters, Muzyka. *Preludes, Etudes, Sonatas.*
- **Prokofiev** — Boosey and Hawkes, Schirmer, Edition Peters. *Sonatas, Toccata, Visions.*
- **Bartók** — Boosey and Hawkes, Editio Musica Budapest. *Mikrokosmos, Suite, Sonata.*
- **Stravinsky** — Boosey and Hawkes. *Petrouchka movements.*
- **Messiaen** — Durand. *Preludes, Vingt regards selected.*
- **Ligeti** — Peters, Schott. *Etudes selected.*
- **Takemitsu** — Schott. *Rain Tree Sketch selected.*

## Public domain strategy

- Use IMSLP for composers who are public domain in your region.
- Compare free edition against a recording and a modern urtext if possible.
- Do not use a bad free edition just because it is free.
- Good fingerings and clean engraving save practice time.

## What to avoid

- obscure contest solos with no standard edition
- weak arrangements of famous orchestral music
- anonymous internet transcriptions without preview
- living composers who are hard to source unless you love them
- pieces chosen only because they appear on an exam list

## Anime and game music

- use official scores
- licensed arrangements from Yamaha, Shinko Music, and similar publishers
- use community transcriptions only after checking against recording
- keep this in the fun slot, not primary technical training
