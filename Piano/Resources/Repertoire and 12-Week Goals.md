# Repertoire and 12-Week Goals

> The structural rule: maintain at least two concurrent pieces.
> This prevents one difficult piece from blocking all progress, and it makes interleaving natural.

## Always maintain

- 1 learning piece — at level, actively teaching the current technical goals
- 1 polishing piece — mostly learned; musical and performance work
- Maintenance pool — previously completed, in Anki rotation
- Optional: 1 easy or fun piece — below level, no perfection required

Two concurrent pieces must never become two concurrent obligations.

## Piece folders and logs

For each serious piece, create a folder:

```text
Pieces/Composer/Piece Title/
  Piece Title.md
  Piece Title - Error Log.md
  Piece Title - Tempo Log.md
  daily/
```

Use piece folders for:

- learning pieces
- polishing pieces
- pieces expected to stay active for more than 2–3 weeks

Do not create piece folders for:

- tiny method-book exercises
- throwaway sight-reading
- one-off fun experiments

See [Pieces README](../Pieces/README.md) for full conventions.

## Choosing repertoire

Primary source:

- RCM Celebration Series Repertoire at your current level

Supplement with:

- IMSLP for public domain repertoire
- your method book while active
- Japanese and anime sources, using licensed or reputable arrangements
- teacher suggestions
- pieces you love

## Difficulty per slot

- Learning piece: at level. Below level will not stretch you; far above level will stall.
- Polishing piece: at or slightly below level.
- Fun or easy piece: below level. This is the pressure valve.

## Functional/ear-playing slot

In addition to classical repertoire slots, maintain one functional project:

- Current functional project: one anime/J-pop song being learned by ear or from a lead sheet
- This is independent of classical stage
- It lives in the Ear and Fun slots of the daily note
- It does not need a piece folder unless it will stay active for more than 3 weeks
- Log it in the daily note with one journal line

## Biomechanical goal per cycle

Each 12-week cycle should include one biomechanical focus:

- Example: "This cycle, focus on wrist alignment during arpeggios"
- Example: "This cycle, focus on economy of motion in scale passages"
- Example: "This cycle, focus on octave wrist flexibility"
- Write it in the 12-Week Goal template under technical goals

## Balance

Aim for variety across:

- periods: Baroque, Classical, Romantic, Contemporary
- characters: fast/slow, loud/quiet, lyrical/rhythmic

Do not pick three pieces of the same type.

## Difficulty check

If you cannot make progress on the first page of a learning piece after two weeks of honest work, it may be mis-leveled.

## Shelving Pieces

Shelve without guilt if:

- blocked 3+ weeks despite plateau-breaking
- you no longer feel motivated by it
- it turns out to be mis-leveled

Shelving is data, not failure.

Return in three months and it may go quickly. Or never return. That is fine too.
